
    Deep Residual Learning for Image Recognition and Neural Network Optimization.
    Abstract:
    Deeper neural networks are more difficult to train. We present a residual learning framework to ease the training of networks that are substantially deeper than those used previously.
    We explicitly reformulate the layers as learning residual functions with reference to the layer inputs, instead of learning unreferenced functions.
    We evaluate our residual nets on the ImageNet dataset with a depth of up to 152 layers.
    An ensemble of these residual nets achieves a 3.57% error on the ImageNet test set, winning 1st place in the ILSVRC 2015 classification task.
    In computer vision and machine learning applications, deep neural architectures have delivered state of the art accuracy.
